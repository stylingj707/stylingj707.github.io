Privacy policy

Iris-Agent and Curio are private apps. The person who built them is the only person who uses them.

When the owner signs in with Google, an app reads only what the sign-in screen lists. Curio reads the owner's YouTube likes and playlists, read only, and keeps them on the owner's own device.

What an app reads is used only for the owner's own use. It is never sold.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
